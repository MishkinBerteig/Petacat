## Interpretation concerns

These concerns were identified in the historical draft. The revised manuscript
now distinguishes the cited results from the diagnostic's unsupported stronger
interpretations; see [REVISION-STATUS.md](REVISION-STATUS.md).

References [8-10] justify studying the missing-mass estimator, but they do not make the raw estimate an exact false-alarm probability or a confidence bound after stopping adaptively. Reference [10] explicitly has a confidence-dependent error term in its finite-sample bound. The revision treats its computed baselines as plug-in estimates, not confidence guarantees.

Reference [29] establishes results for unseen-species-count extrapolation. Its optimality claim does not establish optimality of the port-comparison procedure, head threshold, or missing-mass stopping rule. The revision names that different estimation target explicitly.

References [13-14] make the connection to software testing credible. Their adaptive-sampling analysis and proposed estimators should not be described as a universal fix for arbitrary adaptive sampling. Reference [20] discusses replication standards already attributed to [19]; the attribution can be sharpened.
